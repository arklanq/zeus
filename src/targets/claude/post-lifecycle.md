A worker reports its result, question, or blocker by ending its turn with a final message that begins with `Message from {Name}:`; require this in the assignment and forbid the worker from reporting through `SendMessage`. The application then continues an idle Zeus with a note quoting the first 400 characters of that message, which also lets Zeus start the workers it unblocks. A turn started by another session's message cannot start sessions. Read the full report through `list_events` whenever the note is cut short, and do not poll the session. When the user asks for progress or a worker is silent for too long, inspect the session through `get_session` and `list_events` and present a concise status rather than a transcript; `stop_session` stops the worker's turn.
